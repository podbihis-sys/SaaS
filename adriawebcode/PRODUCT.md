# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses in two distinct markets: the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and the western Balkans (Croatia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro). They are evaluating a web agency, often on a phone during a workday. Many have an outdated site or none at all; they want to know three things fast: is this agency good, what does it cost, how quickly can it happen. The site serves five languages (de/en/hr/bs/sr) and prices by the visitor's market.

## Product Purpose

adriawebcode is a web-design agency founded 2024. The site is its lead engine: visitors fill a form and receive an instant fixed-price quote on screen and by e-mail (with live analysis of their existing site), can accept the offer with one click, and choose a three-tier maintenance plan. Hosting is deliberately not offered. Success = qualified leads who trust the agency enough to accept an automated quote.

## Brand Personality

Präzise, mediterran-selbstbewusst, handwerklich. The agency bridges German engineering expectations and Adriatic ease — "adria" is in the name. The site must *prove* craft by being craft: a client should look at it and think "I want my site to feel like this." Confident, direct, never corporate-generic.

## Anti-references

- Classic AI-generated design (the user's explicit words: "nicht das klassische KI Design"): uniform card grids, gradient-text headlines, glassmorphism panels, uppercase tracked eyebrow labels above every section, hero-metric stat rows, aurora/particle backgrounds.
- Generic SaaS landing pages (Stripe/Linear clones) — this is a *human agency*, not a dev-tool startup.
- Cheap local-agency templates (sliders, stock handshake photos, bullet-list "Warum wir?" sections).

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the portfolio.** Every section must demonstrate the craft being sold; if a section would look at home in a template marketplace, it fails.
2. **One idea per fold.** Deliberate pacing over dense feature grids; the visitor is making one decision (trust), not comparing specs.
3. **Prices are content, not decoration.** The instant-quote flow is the product's sharpest differentiator — the design should stage it like a product demo, not bury it behind marketing prose.
4. **Two markets, one voice.** The design must feel equally credible in Stuttgart and Sarajevo — no cultural-cliché palettes; localization carries the market fit.
5. **Motion earns its place.** Choreographed, physical, purposeful — never fade-on-scroll applied uniformly to everything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible focus states, full keyboard navigation of the quote form, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Five-language content means headings must survive long German compound words without overflow.
